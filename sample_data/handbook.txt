7. ACADEMIC ELIGIBILITY RULES
- Eligibility for Course Registration: A student is eligible to register for a course if and only if the student has completed all prerequisites of the course.
- Eligibility for Examinations: A student is eligible to take the course examination if and only if the student maintains an attendance of at least 75% in that course. Students with attendance below 75% are barred from examinations.
- Eligibility for Capstone Project AI499: A student is eligible for Capstone Project AI499 if the student has completed AI401 and has earned at least 90 credits.
- Eligibility for Dean's Honour List: A student qualifies for the Dean's Honour List if the student attains a CGPA of at least 3.75 with a minimum of 15 completed credits.
- Academic Probation: A student is placed on academic probation if their cumulative CGPA falls below 2.0.
