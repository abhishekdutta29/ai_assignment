Key Strengths of the Symbolic Paradigm

  • Absolute Determinism: Given the same document and query, KnowledgeLens produces the identical mathematical answer and reasoning path every single run ($T=0$).
  • Complete Auditability: In mission-critical academic administration (graduation eligibility, exam clearance, probation), students and faculty require legally defensible justifications, not probabilistic approximations.
  • Zero Training Data Requirement: KnowledgeLens requires zero annotated corpus, fine-tuning, or compute-heavy pretraining. It functions immediately upon ingesting any structured handbook.
  • Graceful Failure: Through closed-world boundaries, queries outside the domain (e.g. "Who teaches Quantum Physics?") are rejected with exactness rather than generating fabricated course descriptions.

Inherent Limitations & Future Work

  • Syntactic Brittleness: Regex-based extractors require sentences to follow anticipated syntactic structures. Unseen formulations may evade extraction rules.
  • Ontology Engineering Overhead: Expanding to new domains (e.g. medical handbooks or corporate compliance) requires engineering new regex patterns and relation lexicons.
  • Paraphrase Sensitivity: While Levenshtein edit distance successfully resolves typos and spelling variants, deep semantic paraphrasing requires rule expansion.
  • Neurosymbolic Roadmap: Future work can explore hybrid neurosymbolic models where a frozen language model suggests candidate facts that are strictly verified and executed by our symbolic reasoning engine.
